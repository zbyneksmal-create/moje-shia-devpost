# Moje Shia — Movement Aftercare

Turn the areas treated in a massage session into a small client-facing home movement plan.

## The Unique Kernel
An area selection becomes the same handover plan on a client device through a QR/link, without a client account or personal record.

## Who It's For
A massage therapist handing over general movement reminders at the end of a session, and the client reading them afterward.

## The Core Loop
Select neck, trapezius, shoulders or forearms; review matching movements; create a plan; open the client's link or scan its QR.

## Inspiration & Identity
Reference: the owner's competition prototype at https://moje-shia-devpost.zbynek-smal.chatgpt.site/. Carry forward its dark green palette, restrained light green accents, large headline, body map and two-column therapist surface. This source reconstruction is not a new product direction.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The owner wants to complete one existing Devpost submission without repeatedly rebuilding the working practice application.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Selecting Neck displays exactly two neck movements. The created client link opens those same two movements, including repetition guidance and stop conditions. Selecting all four groups produces eight movements with no duplicate area group.

## The POC Boundary
Four area groups, eight existing movement texts, therapist selection/preview, handover QR/link, client reading view, and readable source/planning documents. All content is demonstration/general aftercare, not real client data.

## Later
No additional feature is proposed as part of this submission.

## Explicitly Cut
- Real clients, visits, bookings, recordings and imports: unrelated to the small proof and protected production data.
- Runtime AI or diagnosis: the visible matching flow is deterministic.
- Production deployment or database changes: outside the authorized scope.
